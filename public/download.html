Opening the app the first time

Windows: run the installer. It updates itself after that.

Mac: the app isn’t signed with a paid Apple certificate. Right-click Gather and choose Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/Gather.app in Terminal. Pick Apple Silicon for M-series Macs and Intel for older ones.

Linux: run chmod +x Gather-*.AppImage, then open it. Or install the .deb on Debian and Ubuntu.

Mac and Linux are previews and don’t update themselves yet. Come back here for new versions.
