Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026.

The short version

Gather asks for a name and nothing else: no email, phone number or password. We don’t run ads, sell data or track you across the web.

What we store

  • Your profile: the name you pick, an optional picture, and sign-in tokens for your devices.
  • Your chats: messages, reactions, files and pictures you send, and which chats you belong to.
  • Abuse-prevention logs: the IP address and time for sign-ups, file uploads and reports. These are kept about 90 days, then deleted, and are used only to stop abuse and act on reports.
  • Reports: what you report and a short excerpt, kept until the report is handled.

Who can see it

People in a chat can see what is sent there. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted, so the person running the service could technically read them, and we will look at content when it is reported or needed to keep the service safe or to meet a legal duty. We may share information with the authorities when the law requires it or to report illegal content.

Services we rely on

  • Cloudflare hosts the app, stores data and files, and relays voice calls when a direct connection isn’t possible. Calls otherwise go directly between friends.
  • Klipy powers GIF search. Search words you type are sent to Klipy to fetch results.

How long we keep things

Files are removed from the server after days to weeks (sooner when the service is busy). Messages are kept much longer, and may be removed at any time. Your device also keeps its own copy of chats you’ve seen.

Your choices

You can delete your own messages in the app. To have your account or chat data removed, or to ask what we hold, use the report page or Discord link below. Gather isn’t meant for children under 13.

Questions, reports or takedown requests: use the report page, the Report button on any message in the app, or ask in our Discord.
